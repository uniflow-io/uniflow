Uniflow Meets FrankenPHP: A New Era for PHP Apps 🚀
We're excited to announce that Uniflow now runs on FrankenPHP — a blazing-fast ⚡️, modern application server for PHP.

FrankenPHP brings native performance improvements, async support, and built-in worker capabilities that supercharge the way we serve your flows. It's everything we love about PHP, reimagined for the modern web.

Why did we switch?
Because performance, reliability, and simplicity matter. FrankenPHP helps us deliver your automations faster, more efficiently, and with greater flexibility.

Let’s keep the flows smooth and the servers lean.
Happy flowing! 🎉
